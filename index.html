Hassan Mokari

Hi, I’m Hassan. I am a Research Assistant in Electrical Engineering at Florida Atlantic University (FAU), working under the supervision of Dr. Yufei Tang and Dr. James VanZwieten. My work focuses on Energy Systems, Control Systems, Optimization, Modeling and Simulation, and Data-Driven Control Co-Design using Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning, with applications to physics-informed engineering systems and renewable energy technologies. This work has resulted in peer-reviewed journal and conference papers.

My first research priority is Control Co-Design for Energy Harvesting and Conversion, where I jointly study physical design and closed-loop control for wave energy converters and ocean current turbines. I also develop Scientific Machine Learning, Digital Twins, and Physics-Informed Systems by combining physical models, numerical simulation, data-driven methods, and optimization. My methods include optimal control, nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC), MATLAB/Simulink, genetic algorithms, Bayesian optimization, reinforcement learning, machine learning, and deep learning.

In Robotics and Autonomous Systems, I work on IoT-based Resilient Connected Vehicles, including attack detection, distributed control, and stability of vehicle platoons under cyberattacks. During my master’s degree, I also worked on medical and rehabilitation robotics, including a laparoscopic suturing robot and a wrist rehabilitation robot with torque regulation. I also develop Ocean Current Turbine Models and Controllers to study motion regulation, energy generation, and robustness under changing ocean conditions. I welcome opportunities that connect advanced control and optimization with real-world engineering applications.

Selected Engineering Projects

01 / Energy systems

Control co-design for energy harvesting and conversion

Jointly optimizing physical design and closed-loop control for dual-rotor ocean current turbines and a three-paddle wave energy converter. The work uses simulation-driven digital twins to investigate geometry, buoyancy regulation, PTO damping, active torque control, and operating limits to improve energy conversion and system behavior.

Finite-horizon control · Nonlinear MPC · Genetic algorithms · Bayesian optimization

Scientific ML & physics-informed systems

Building physics-informed digital twins by integrating dynamic models, numerical simulation, data analysis, and machine learning to evaluate complex engineering systems. Reinforcement learning has been used alongside optimization-based controllers in ocean energy design studies.

MATLAB/Simulink · Python · Machine learning · Data analysis

Resilient connected vehicles

Developed attack-detection and distributed-control methods for autonomous vehicle platoons subject to denial-of-service and deception attacks. Studied compromised agents, changing communication topology, leader switching, and recovery of platoon stability.

Laparoscopic suturing robot

Wrist rehabilitation robot

Ocean current turbine modeling & control

Developed a nonlinear MATLAB/Simulink digital twin of a 1.2-MW-class dual-rotor turbine with rotors, generators, buoyancy actuators, and hydrodynamic loading. Evaluated motion control and robustness under measured ocean-current profiles; contributed to grid-integrated hardware-in-the-loop research.
